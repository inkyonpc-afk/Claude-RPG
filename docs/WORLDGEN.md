# Worldgen

## Terrain stack (one terrain generator, no conflicts)
**Tectonic + Terralith** (via TerraBlender/Lithostitched) plus Oh The Biomes We've Gone and Oh The Trees You'll Grow; Alex's Caves for deep cave biomes; Underground Rivers; Vanilla Backport content; Nether: Incendium, Nether Depths Upgrade, Gardens of the Dead, Bygone Nether, Formations Nether; End: Nullscape, End Remastered, YUNG's End Island, MES (Moog's End Structures). Rejected: BetterEnd/BetterNether (Fabric-only), second terrain generators (Biomes O' Plenty, Regions Unexplored kept as test-status only), Every Compat (registry mismatches).

## Dimensions (all load; portal order enforced by quests + Restricted Portals in Phase 7)
Twilight Forest, Undergarden, Aether (+Deep Aether, Aether Redux, Lost Content, Ancient Aether), Blue Skies (Everbright, Everdawn), Deeper and Darker (Otherside), Iron's Spells pocket dimension, Nether, End.

## Structures (about 1,600 registered; tiers)
| Tier | Examples | Intended loot |
|---|---|---|
| Common | camps, small ruins, minor caves, mineshafts, villages | supplies, common gear |
| Uncommon | towers, forts, village variants, Dungeons Arise towers | uncommon gear, gems |
| Rare | dungeons, castles, large temples (Dungeons Enhanced, Prodigium, Better Strongholds) | rare/epic gear |
| Very rare | mega-dungeons, boss fortresses (Keep Kayra, Black Citadel, Cataclysm arenas) | epic/mythic, boss gear |

Spacing is tuned with **Structurify** (per-structure spacing/separation/frequency) and Structure Essentials / Sparse Structures Reforged; goal: adventure without every structure spawning in sight of another. Known: `Non-unique structure_set salt:0` warning from Structure Essentials (cosmetic).

## Performance note
Registry size: ~31k items, ~22k blocks (decor mods dominate). Startup: ~150 s to world on a Ryzen 9 5900X. See PERFORMANCE.md.

## Known issues
Prodigium Dungeons ships 2 empty structure files (blocked by `aldreth_fixes` datapack). Structure-tag/loot-table references to removed mods log harmless "Load My Tags" errors.

## Structure spacing tiers (generated by tools/tune_structures.py)
Sparse Structures Reforged multiplies each set's spacing and separation; Structurify is installed but neutral (one spacing system only). Strongholds use rings and are unaffected. Targets are starts per km2 (1000x1000 blocks) in the overworld.

Factors are **calibrated from a measured census** (`tools/structure_census.py` over 6.93 km2 of generated overworld, `tools/calibrate_spacing.py`): density scales as 1/factor^2, so each tier's factor is sqrt(measured density at factor 1 / target density).

| Tier | Factor | Target /km2 | Sets | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| realm | 1.00x | n/a | 40 | dimension-designed layouts (Twilight Forest, Aether family, Blue Skies, Undergarden, Otherside, End); untouched |
| boss | 2.55x | 0.4 | 40 | boss arenas and quest targets: rare, but findable with a map and a plan |
| settlement | 1.40x | 1.5 | 24 | villages and towns: rest stops, traders, bounties |
| great | 2.35x | 0.5 | 38 | mega-dungeons: an expedition each |
| vanilla | 2.50x | 3.0 | 18 | vanilla structures (mansions, monuments, ruins, wrecks, ruined portals) and their replacements |
| dungeon | 3.55x | 4.0 | 186 | medium dungeons, towers and camps: the bread and butter of exploration |
| ocean_decor | 4.50x | n/a | 19 | biome-bound sea decor (arches, spirals, sunken villages, rafts, wrecks): fixed factor, excluded from calibration because it is biome-restricted and very prolific |
| clutter | 4.25x | 12.0 | 475 | small ruins, wells, cabins and decor: spaced so discoveries feel deliberate |
| default | 1.40x | 3.0 | 19 | anything unlisted (new mods default here) |

## Spacing: how it was measured (2026-10-06)
- **Method:** `tools/structure_census.py` reads the region files of a generated world and counts structure starts per tier; `tools/calibrate_spacing.py` turns counts into factors (density scales as 1/factor squared).
- **Sample 1:** 4.1 km2 around spawn (an ocean and ice-heavy area), generated with the first-pass factors. Showed ocean decor (Aquamirae arches and spirals, sunken villages) swamping every land tier, which is why `ocean_decor` is a separate fixed tier now.
- **Sample 2:** 2.8 km2 in four regions 7,000 blocks out, generated under the calibrated first-pass factors: dungeon 4.6/km2 (target 4), vanilla 3.2 (target 3), settlement 1.8 (target 1.5), clutter 20 (target 12, still dense). Boss-tier structures did not appear at all in 2.8 km2, as intended for a 0.4/km2 target.
- **Final factors** (table above) pool both samples; tiers with few samples are blended toward their priors. They are not re-verified by a third world: treat them as a good first calibration, and re-run the census after any mod change.
- **Locate caveat:** `/locate structure minecraft:village_plains` found a village 5 km away in the test world. That is biome gating (Terralith and Tectonic make plains rare), not spacing; story quests never depend on a specific structure (structure quests are optional).
