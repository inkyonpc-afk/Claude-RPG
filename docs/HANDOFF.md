## 7. Quest book: root cause found and fixed in the generator (2026-10-07); confirm in-game first
**Root cause of "chapters don't open":** the chapters did open, but every map looked empty. FTB Quests places a chapter image by its CENTER (`QuestPanel.alignWidgets`) and opens a chapter centered on the bounding box of quests and images (`resetScroll`). `build_quests.py` wrote each 30x17-unit backdrop's top-left corner into `x`/`y`, so the box and the opening view sat up-left of the quests. At 1280x720 (GUI scale 3) 26 of 29 chapters opened with no quest on screen. The backdrop was also invisible (`alpha: 0.55d`; `ChapterImage.readData` reads an int, so 0). Pre-fix screenshots never showed a chapter for this reason, and zooming or scrolling out would have revealed the quests.
**Certain Questing Additions** was not a fault: with its default `panel_button_hover` it draws the selected chapter as "Name ◀" in gray instead of FTB's white frame (that was the "click does nothing" look). It does not touch quest sync or the opening scroll, so it stays (it is on both sides, see the handshake note in KNOWN_ISSUES).
**Fixed at the same time (all silent in game, found in FTB's source):** 17 cross-chapter dependency ids pointed at no quest; 17 dimension tasks used key `dim` (never completable; one grants the flight stage); 193 command rewards used `elevate` instead of `elevate_perms` (failed for non-ops); 47 quests still waited on optional structure hunts. Quest ids are unchanged, so existing progress is kept.
**Confirm in a running client (not done yet):**
1. `python tools/verify.py` (expect 44/44 with jars installed).
2. `python tools/launch_client.py --world AldrethTest01 --wait 240 --xmx 10G --size 1280x720 --script "j62:key j;j72:shot qbook_open"`: the Prologue opens on its 12 quests with the backdrop behind them at about 55 % opacity. Then open the chapter list and click two or three chapters (as in run `g34`) and take shots: each should open on its quests.
3. As a non-op player, complete a quest with a command reward (e.g. "The Constellation of Embers": 1 skill point) and a dimension quest (enter the Nether: "The Land Below" in Act II, or "Flame" in the Realms chapter), and confirm "The Road Calls" completes without a village.
If the map is still empty in step 2, look in the client log for `[STDERR]` stack traces from `Panel.refreshWidgets` (FTB Library catches exceptions from `addWidgets` and only prints them), then test without Quests Additions (its source is not public and it injects into FTB's Quest class; the campaign uses none of its features).
