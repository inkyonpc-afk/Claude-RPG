# Races

_Generated by `tools/build_origins.py`. Each people has a small identity bonus and a real drawback; there is no class lock (see SKILL_TREE.md). Flight-granting default origins (Elytrian, Phantom, Avian) are removed on purpose._

## Emberborn Human

_Adaptable and driven: the Ember burns brightest in those who never settle._

- **Adaptable**: You learn faster than the other peoples (+10% experience).
- **Fortunate Birth**: A little luck follows the Emberborn.

## Sylvan Elf

_Forest-keepers of old Aldreth: swift, keen and slight of frame._

- **Keen Senses**: +4% dodge chance and +10% bow draw speed.
- **Wildspeaker**: +5% nature spell power.
- **Slender Frame**: -2 maximum health.

## Stoneborn Dwarf

_Delvers under the Hollow Crown's mountains: sturdy, stubborn and tireless miners._

- **Stoneskin**: +2 armor and +10% knockback resistance.
- **Master Delver**: +15% mining speed.
- **Short Stride**: -6% movement speed.

## Ashen Orc

_Hardened by the ashlands: strong, hardy and impatient with spellcraft._

- **Ashen Might**: +2 attack damage and +4 maximum health.
- **Thick Skull**: +5% knockback resistance.
- **Dull to Magic**: -10% spell power.

## Hearthfolk Halfling

_Small, quick and unreasonably lucky: they find fortune where others find trouble._

- **Lucky Foot**: +2 luck.
- **Nimble**: +3% dodge chance.
- **Small Frame**: -4 maximum health.

## Hexblood

_Descendants of a banished coven: fire in the veins, holy light a burn._

- **Infernal Gift**: +10% fire spell power and +15% fire magic resistance.
- **Cursed Line**: -15% holy magic resistance.
- **Frail Plating**: -1 armor.

## Scaleborn

_Thin-blooded kin of the old dragons: armored, proud and heavy._

- **Scaled Hide**: +3 armor and +2 armor toughness.
- **Draconic Blood**: +20% fire magic resistance and +5% fire spell power.
- **Heavy Frame**: -8% movement speed and -20% swim speed.

## Wisp-touched

_Spirit-kissed wanderers, half in the world and half in the Veil._

- **Veilborn Mind**: +25 maximum mana and +8% mana regeneration.
- **Flicker**: +3% dodge chance.
- **Insubstantial**: -2 armor.
